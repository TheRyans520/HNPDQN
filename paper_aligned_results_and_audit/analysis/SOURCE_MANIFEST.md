names are case-insensitive, the legacy uppercase `Definitions/figures/F3.pdf`
was renamed `F3_uppercase_legacy.pdf`; the lowercase `f3.pdf` referenced by the
LaTeX source retained its original name.

copied to `sources/original_round1_template.tex` (SHA-256
`7AE781F75B8334C48B364A2C58B33938D9C265D07154427C3E3F3A3E7FF6DABF`).

extracted into `experiment_v2/data/raw/`: eight jammer centre frequencies and
ten scan files for each of 20 cm / 10 dBm, 40 cm / 10 dBm, and 20 cm / 5 dBm.
No legacy model or result file is treated as confirmatory evidence.
